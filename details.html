PAGEFORMATTING

Page Formatting is the backbone of any professional document, ensuring it is both readable and visually balanced

STRUCTURAL LAYOUT

The structural layout defines the "skeleton" of the page

MARGINGS: This is the white space surrounding the text

Orientation: most documents use portrait(vertical), which is ideal for letters. landscape(horizontal) is resevered for wide tables, charts, or visual presentations.

Page Size: Depending on your region, this usually letter (8.5 x 11) in North America or A4(210mm x 297mm) internationally

Headers and Footers:These areas sit within the top and bottom margins.

TEXTUAL TYPOGRAPHY AND SPACING

Once the boundaries are set, textual formatting ensures the information is degestible for yhe reader

Line spacing: this refers to the vertical gap between lines of text

Alignment: left Alignment is the most readable for long form text.

Justified(stretched to both edges) creates a formal "block" look often seen in newspapers or textbooks

Indentation: usually applied to the first line of paragraph or "hanging" for biblographies

Hierarachy : Using different font sizes and weights(Bold,Italics) for headings helps the readerscan the document to find specific sections quickly
